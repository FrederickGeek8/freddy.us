## Coffee Chats
If you might be interested in sitting down for a virtual coffee chat, you can book time with me <a href="https://cal.com/frederickm" rel="external" target="_blank" class="link">on my calendar</a>. My door is open if you want to ask advice, pitch ideas, or speak about collaborating. Other topics are okay, but regardless I ask that you fill out the topic you wish to discuss in the form while you are booking time.

If you are a business that is looking for a consultant to help you scope out your future of integrating AI or Machine Learning into your business, or deciding whether you want to pursue these technologies at all, feel free to contact me. With [my experience pioneering a company's AI frontier](/resume/#hinalea-imaging) and my [considerate philosophy on when "AI" is useful for a problem](/blog/), I would love to help your business make an educated decision on how to adopt these technologies.
