Attending the 2026 Joint Mathematics Meeting this week? I created an in-browser tool to convert the personal agenda you designed in the JMM mobile app to a iCal calendar format. You can import the resulting file into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and more!

Check it out here: [jmmcal.freddy.us](https://jmmcal.freddy.us/)

It's a quick and dirty website, but I thought I'd share anyways! Hopefully it helps someone other than myself.
